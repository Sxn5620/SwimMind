# SwimMind

## Mental Wellness App for Swimmers

SwimMind is a comprehensive mental wellness application designed specifically for swimmers, helping them navigate the psychological challenges of competitive swimming and training.

### Key Features
- Daily mental wellness check-ins with streak tracking
- Specialized meditation and visualization exercises for swimmers
- Community support from fellow athletes
- AI-powered chatbot for personalized mental wellness guidance
- Achievement system to track your mental training progress
