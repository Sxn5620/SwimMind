## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I reset my password?
Visit the Profile section and select "Reset Password" or contact support directly.

### Is my data secure?
SwimMind takes data privacy seriously. All personal information is encrypted and stored securely. See our [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.html) for details.

### How do I delete my account?
To delete your account, go to Profile > Settings > Delete Account, or contact our support team.
